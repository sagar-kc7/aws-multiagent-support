# CloudNest Billing Policies

## Subscription Plans
CloudNest offers three subscription tiers: Starter ($9/month), Pro ($29/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing, contact sales). All plans are billed monthly by default, with an annual option that gives a 20% discount when paid upfront.

## Payment Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Bank transfers are available only for Enterprise customers with an annual contract. We do not accept cryptocurrency at this time.

## Billing Cycle
Your billing date is set to the day you first subscribed. If you upgrade mid-cycle, we charge a prorated amount immediately for the remaining days at the new tier's rate. If you downgrade mid-cycle, the new (lower) rate takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle — we do not issue partial refunds for downgrades.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days, then again after 7 days. After a second failed retry, the account is downgraded to a restricted "Grace" mode for 14 days, during which core features remain accessible but usage limits drop to Starter-tier levels. If payment is not resolved within the 14-day grace period, the account is suspended (not deleted) and can be reactivated any time by updating payment details.

## Invoices and Receipts
Invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing cycle and emailed to the account owner. Past invoices can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoice History. Enterprise customers can request custom invoice formatting (PO numbers, tax IDs) by contacting billing@cloudnest.example.

## Taxes
Sales tax or VAT is calculated based on the billing address on file and added at checkout. Tax-exempt organizations must submit exemption documentation to billing@cloudnest.example before it can be applied.

## Changing Payment Details
Payment methods can be updated at any time from Settings > Billing > Payment Method. Updating your card does not trigger an immediate charge — it takes effect on your next scheduled billing date.

## Multi-Seat Billing
On Pro and Enterprise plans, additional seats are billed at $8/seat/month, prorated for the remainder of the current billing cycle when added. Removing a seat does not generate a refund for the current cycle but reduces the seat count starting next cycle.
