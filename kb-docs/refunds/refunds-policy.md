# CloudNest Refunds & Cancellations Policy

## General Refund Eligibility
CloudNest offers a 14-day money-back guarantee for first-time subscribers on any plan. Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the initial charge via Settings > Billing > Request Refund, or by emailing refunds@cloudnest.example. Refunds are not available for renewal charges after the initial 14-day window, only for the first payment.

## Annual Plan Refunds
Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund if canceled within the first 30 days, calculated as the unused portion of the year minus a $20 processing fee. After 30 days, annual plans are non-refundable but can still be canceled to prevent renewal.

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days, though it may take an additional 3-5 business days for the amount to appear on your statement depending on your bank or card issuer. Refunds are always issued to the original payment method — we cannot issue refunds to a different card or account.

## Cancellation vs. Refund
Canceling a subscription stops future billing but does not automatically refund the current period — you retain access until the end of the paid cycle. To request money back for the current period, a separate refund request is needed and is only guaranteed within the 14-day window above.

## Enterprise Contract Refunds
Enterprise customers on annual contracts follow the terms specified in their signed agreement rather than this general policy. Early termination of an Enterprise contract may incur an early termination fee as specified in the contract; contact your account manager for specifics.

## Disputed Charges
If you believe you were charged in error (duplicate charge, wrong amount, charged after cancellation), contact refunds@cloudnest.example with your invoice number. These are investigated within 2 business days and, if confirmed as an error, refunded in full regardless of the 14-day window.

## Downgrade Refunds
As noted in the billing policy, downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle and do not generate a partial refund for the difference in the current cycle.

## Chargebacks
Customers are asked to contact CloudNest support before initiating a chargeback with their bank, since chargebacks can result in immediate account suspension while the dispute is investigated, which is usually slower than a direct refund request.
